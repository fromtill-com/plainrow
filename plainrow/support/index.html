Do files leave my machine?

No. Files never leave the machine. There is no upload, no account, and no telemetry. We never ask you to send a CSV for support. Describe column names and row count, not contents.

Lite or Kitchen?

Lite is two files: stack, dedupe, and export. It is a smaller product, not a switch. Join, recipes, and split are Kitchen, along with filter, rename, find-replace, and date normalize. Kitchen's cap is about 200,000 rows or 40 MB. License is personal use plus one commercial seat. Do not redistribute the app.

How do I join on a key?

Kitchen only. Open both files, Join, pick the key column on each side. Inner, left, right, and full are available. Lite cannot join.

File too big?

If a Chromebook or a large export complains, split the export or filter in the source tool first, then stack. Kitchen's cap is about 200,000 rows or 40 MB. Lite stays two files, stack and dedupe only.

More than two files?

Lite stays two files. Kitchen is the full engine.

What is the refund policy?

Checkout is not live yet, so there is nothing to refund today. After a Kitchen purchase, you have 14 days, no questions, through the same checkout. No explanation required. After 14 days, refunds are discretionary except we still make it right if the file will not open in a current desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. This policy is for the Software, not the contents of your spreadsheets.

Will you take my files?

No. We never ask you to send a CSV. If something breaks, describe the shape of the file (column names, row count), not the contents.
