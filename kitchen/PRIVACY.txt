PLAINROW KITCHEN — PRIVACY

Plainrow does not collect, upload, or see your files.

- The application is a single HTML file you open on your own computer.
- All processing happens in your browser, on your machine.
- There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud conversion step.
- There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no third-party scripts.
- The file does not require a network connection after you have saved it.
- Cookies are not set by Plainrow.

If you bought the Software through a checkout page, that payment provider
holds the email and payment details needed to deliver the download. That
relationship is theirs, not a Plainrow account, and we do not receive your
spreadsheets.

We do not train models on buyer files. We never ask you to send a CSV
for "support." If something breaks, describe the shape of the file
(column names, row count), not the contents.

A file formatter you run locally. We never see your data.
