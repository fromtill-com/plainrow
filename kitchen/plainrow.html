How this works

Plainrow Kitchen is one HTML file. Open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. You do not install anything and you do not need a network after the file is on disk.

  1. Open one or more CSV (or TSV) files, or load a synthetic demo.
  2. Use the toolbar: stack files, join on a key, split by a column, dedupe, filter, rename or reorder columns, find-replace, clean, sort, or normalize dates.
  3. Recipes on the left run a Monday-job sequence for you. You can also load a recipe JSON.
  4. Export writes a CSV back to your Downloads folder. A UTF-8 BOM is optional for Excel.

Hard cap on the full Kitchen: about 200,000 rows or 40 MB in memory, whichever comes first. Lite is 2 files and 500 rows. Those limits exist so the browser stays usable.

Dates like 3/2/26, 02-Mar-2026, and 2026-03-02 are understood. Ambiguous day/month pairs default to month-first (US).

Your files never leave this computer. There is no telemetry.
