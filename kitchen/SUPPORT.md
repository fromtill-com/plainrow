## How do I join on a key?
Kitchen only. Open both files, Join, pick the key column on each side (inner/left/right/full). Lite cannot join — that is the paid tool.

## Chromebook said the file is too big
Kitchen cap is about 200k rows / 40 MB. Split the export first, or filter in the source tool, then stack. Lite is two files, stack and dedupe only.

## I want more than two files in Lite
Lite is a smaller product, not a switch. Kitchen is the full engine.
